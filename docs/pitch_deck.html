Problem Statement · Slide 2

🚨 The Problem: Alert Fatigue & Blindspots in Healthcare FWA

Who Has the Problem?

  • Healthcare Payers & Insurers: Private payers, Medicare, and Medicaid plans losing an estimated $100B+ annually to fraudulent claims.
  • Special Investigation Units (SIUs): Compliance investigators overwhelmed by massive alert queues with 90%+ false positive rates.
  • Ethical Healthcare Providers: Legitimate clinics facing payment friction, billing audits, and abrasion from naive threshold flags.
  • Patients & Taxpayers: Bearing inflated premiums and substandard care from phantom billers.

Why Existing Systems Fail

  • Alert Firehose: 50k claims trigger 4,000+ isolated flags. Investigators waste days triaging single claims instead of coordinated entities.
  • Single-Claim Blindspot: Inability to detect circular referral rings, shell ownership, or closed-to-reopened Phoenix clinics.
  • Morbidity Ignorance: Legitimate high-acuity clinics are falsely flagged because naive averages ignore patient illness severity.
  • Unexplainable Black Boxes: Opaque risk scores with zero evidentiary defense testing.
Presenter Cue: "The status quo in fraud detection is broken. When a legacy tool flags 4,000 individual claims without context, 95% of an investigator's time is wasted triaging decoys. Worse, legacy tools can't see network-level coordination or distinguish between a true upcoder and a legitimate doctor treating sicker patients."
